1. Torrance Memorial vs. other networksREAL DATA, 2024

How many of the country's doctor networks Torrance Memorial beats on each Medicare quality score. It beats 95% on blood pressure but only 19% on depression. Longer bar = better.

2. The main findingSIMULATED DATA

Patients may be getting better, but they aren't given the survey that proves it. We followed patients with a high depression score for a year. Most adults came back and were checked, but only 12.3% got the full 9-question survey, the only one that can show recovery (red bar). Teens get the full survey at every checkup.

5. Every possible fix, comparedSIMULATED DATA

The three recommended changes are highlighted: cheap, fast, and no new staff. Together they raise the share checked from 70.8% to 80.4%. "After" assumes half of visits use the change; recovery numbers use the most cautious estimate.

6. How we'll know it's workingSIMULATED DATA

Share of yearly checkups that include a depression check, by month. It has stayed around 77% for three years and never gone above 80.5%. After the changes, a month above that line is the first sign they're working.
