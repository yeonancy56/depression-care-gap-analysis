# A3 Improvement Plan: Low Depression Scores

**Torrance Memorial's doctors' network · Medicare quality scores for depression**
Nancy Yeo · Clinical Quality Analyst candidate · October 2026 · PDF version: [`A3_Depression_Remission_Gap_Nancy_Yeo.pdf`](A3_Depression_Remission_Gap_Nancy_Yeo.pdf)

### 1. Why this matters
Medicare scores Torrance Memorial's doctors' network on quality. Better scores mean the network keeps more of the money it saves Medicare (it earned **$5.15 million** in 2024). Mental health care is also one of Torrance Memorial's community goals for 2025–2028.

### 2. Where we are now, and the goal *(real data, 2024)*
| Measure | Torrance Memorial | Typical network |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Depressed patients counted as recovered after a year | **7 in 100** | 14 in 100 |
| Patients checked for depression (and followed up) | **74 in 100** | 84 in 100 |
| Blood pressure under control | **91 in 100 (top 5%)** | 80 in 100 |

The blood-pressure result shows the team can fix a gap. Depression is the next one to fix.
**Goal:** within one year, match the typical network nationally: **14 in 100** recovered and **84 in 100** checked.

### 3. Why it happens *(simulated data)*
> **Patients may be getting better, but they aren't given the survey that proves it.**

There's a **short 2-question** depression survey and a **full 9-question** one. Only the full survey can show a patient recovered. Adults usually get only the short one; teens get the full one at every checkup. Counted as recovered after a year: **adults 2%, teens 67%**.

**Also:** half of missed depression checks happened at **yearly checkups**, and young adults (18–34) are checked least because many skip checkups.

### 4. The fix: three small system changes
1. **Give the full survey a year later** to anyone who had a high score before.
2. **Require a depression check at every yearly checkup.**
3. **Add a box to record the score** at pregnancy visits.

In the simulation, these raise the share of patients checked from **71% to 80%**, even if only half of visits use them. They need no new staff.

### 5. Plan
| When | What |
| --- | --- |
| Weeks 1–2 | Check Torrance Memorial's real records: do adults mostly get the short survey? |
| Weeks 3–6 | Build the three changes with IT. Try them at 1–2 clinics. |
| Months 2–3 | Track results monthly. If they work, roll out to all clinics. |
| Months 3–6 | Reach young adults: check for depression at any visit, not just checkups. |

### 6. How we'll know it's working
- **Monthly:** share of checkups that include a depression check. It's been steady around 77% for three years. **A month above 80%** is the first sign it's working.
- **Monthly:** share of past high scorers who get the full survey a year later.
- **Every quarter:** the two Medicare depression scores.

### Risks
- **Too many pop-up alerts** annoy staff, so alerts should only appear for patients who are due.
- **If adults already get the full survey,** the problem is the treatment itself, and the next step is more mental-health support.
